We are glad you found us. This site is the new home for everything Clearview Consulting: what we do, who we work with, and the lessons we pick up along the way.

## Why a blog?

Most of our best advice never made it past a client call or a whiteboard. Writing it down forces us to be clearer, and it lets us share what works with businesses we have not met yet.

Expect short, practical posts — the kind you can read over a coffee and act on the same week. Topics we have queued up:

- How to know when a process is worth automating
- The one-page operations review we run with every new client
- Hiring your first operations lead without breaking payroll

## Stay in touch

We publish about once a month. If something here raises a question about your own business, [get in touch](/about/) — the first conversation is always free.
